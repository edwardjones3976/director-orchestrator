7. Direction proposal for the owner's macro approval

In one line: since 2026-09-29 the project has built a trust chain and a diagnostic ladder instead of a dataset and an adapter. The data route is defensible under the provider terms, but its heaviest machinery exists to tolerate a host that mis-reads bytes and a student that changed twice in one day.

The Director recommends the simple path first.

  1. Host integrity: repair, not tolerate. The admin memory test of 2026-10-22 reported a one-byte read flip on two drives. Have the machine diagnosed and repaired as a normal PC fault (memory, NVMe, chipset driver) before more GPU time is spent, and keep the two-consecutive-read rule as the check. The alternative is to declare the verified-read loader sufficient and stop extending it. The Director recommends the repair; engineering around the fault is what consumed October. This is the owner's machine and the owner's call.
  2. Student: the M1 winner first. Train the first adapter on Qwen3-14B at 40c0698. The 2026-09-29 probe reserved 10.4 GiB on short rows (D-M2-14, D-M2-19); run the D-M1-F probe once on real constructed rows. Fall back to the bf16 Qwen3-8B, already pinned under manifest v3, only if that probe fails the 11 GiB gate. Drop the 4-bit pin and its unapproved manifest v4 unless the owner wants it kept as a third fallback.
  3. Data: run the approved teacher pilot now, and decide the provider question once. Run the Route R pilot that revision 8 already approved (12 canonical rows, both graders, at least 4 of 12 to proceed, USD 1.50 cap, D-M2-33) with the merged tooling, then scale to the 96-row batch of D-M2-41. In parallel the owner decides the provider-exemplar question in one of three ways: (i) keep provider outputs out of training, the status quo; (ii) ask Anthropic in writing for permission for this private, non-commercial use, which the help centre names as the route; (iii) decide that a private local tool does not "compete" and reopen the M2-G2 provider mix, accepting the terms risk. The Director recommends (i) now with (ii) started in parallel, because written permission removes the dispute entirely.
  4. Trust chain: freeze. One installed root, one owner-approved manifest, no new roots until a trained adapter exists. Keep the two-consecutive-read rule and the existing leak wall.
  5. Dates: re-baseline after the pilot. L1 on 2026-11-12 is not reachable. Under O-022 the date slips and the bar holds; the Director will propose dates within one week of the pilot result.
  6. Set B: decide the size for L1. Zero of 50 frozen at a required pace of 17.5 items a week is not credible. Either a 20-item prospective Set B qualifies L1 with the per-item bar unchanged and the full 50 required for L2, or L1 scores Set A plus the historical reconstructed set as a disclosed proxy. Owner decision.

Decisions requested from the owner. (1) Host: repair, or tolerate with the loader as is. (2) Student: 14B first with bf16 8B fallback, or keep the 4-bit 8B. (3) Provider outputs: (i), (ii) or (iii) above. (4) Set B size for L1. (5) Re-baseline the dates after the pilot. (6) Whether OpenAI Codex stays the maintenance writer for this project's trainer and tooling, or that maintenance moves to Cursor or Claude by Director assignment; the charter routes maintenance to Codex, and "I don't like OpenAI's direction" may or may not include its labour.
