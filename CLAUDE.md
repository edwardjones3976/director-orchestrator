# Claude lane instructions

## INVESTIGATOR responsibility

OpenAI/Director team members (ChatGPT and Codex) also act as **INVESTIGATOR**:
proactively identify issues while other providers work, document evidence,
impact, uncertainty, and suggested solutions, then communicate a bounded
handoff to the responsible provider. Track ownership and resolution evidence;
do not directly fix an investigated issue or take over the ongoing assignment.
Use existing approval, safety, budget, and independent-review gates.

See the [team operating charter](docs/team-operating-charter.md#openaidirector--investigator) for the finding and handoff requirements.


This lane has no standing director role. Read `AGENTS.md` and
`docs/project-leadership.md` for capability-based routing boundaries and
recorded runtime coordination status.

Complete the bounded work in the current assignment. Return evidence, blockers
and proposed next steps to the ChatGPT/Codex Director. Coordination or delegation
requires explicit assignment authority; this provider instruction creates none.
Preserve one writer per file, verified capabilities, independence, availability,
budget and the current allocation profile. Do not require every lane to
participate in every task.

Keep the owner informed with outcomes, evidence, blockers, and the next action.
Reuse fresh evidence; do not restart completed audits or unavailable routes.
Keep all exact-SHA, CI, security, authorship, workload, and spending controls.
Never claim a leadership handoff, green CI, a staged adapter, or a paid-off
simulation proves live provider execution.

This file changes project instructions. It does not change your actual model,
grant credentials, authorize self-acceptance, or start a deployment.
