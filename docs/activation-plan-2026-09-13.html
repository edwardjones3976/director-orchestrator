LangGraph-primary + n8n activation plan (v4 — ASAP)

Objective: earliest completion. Outer bound: Sunday 2026-09-13 07:00 PDT — a no later than, not a due date. Target: Wednesday 2026-09-09 evening PDT; conservative Thursday midday. Issued: 2026-09-08 19:00 PDT · Revised: 2026-09-09 02:10 PDT Issued by: Claude, acting Director-Orchestrator Owner: Ed Jones (edwardjones3976)

v3 supersedes v2. It adopts the seven Director decision rules in docs/director-decision-rules.html as governing for this delivery, and applies them: the schedule, the gate ledger and the work orders below are the output of those rules, not merely compatible with them. It retains v2's five owner decisions and the deployment topology revision.

Everything here is a plan. Nothing in it is activation evidence, and no control action in it has been run.

0. Operating rules (governing)

Adopted by owner direction on 2026-09-08 and recorded in full in docs/director-decision-rules.html. Every lane applies them without asking. They remove no deterministic gate, relax no approval and create no authority; where a rule and AGENTS.md or src/director_orchestrator/policy.py disagree, the gate wins.

Rule In one line What it already changed in this plan
R1 Sequence by p_fail / cost_to_determine Check the most-likely-to-fail, cheapest-to-check condition first Gate 8 is determined Wednesday, not at the Saturday go/no-go. Every cheaply-determined gate is resolved before any expensively-satisfied one
R2 Three-valued ledger Gates are T/F/U; completion is computed, never asserted §7 is now a T/F/U ledger with a derived completion line and a separate no-go disjunction
R3 Name the decision it flips An optional action with no flippable decision has zero value and is not taken Added to the stop rules; every work order states the decision its investigation serves
R4 Temporal network, not critical path Min lags and max lags; feasible iff no negative cycle New WO-14 runs the check before Thursday. ValidateProviders is deliberately scheduled late, not early
R5 Operator-minutes is the binding resource Σ attended durations ≤ Σ window minutes, capacity one WO-14 sums the attended estimates against ~14.5 h of windows. Over budget means scope moves, never "add an agent"
R6 Evidence carries a binding key Reuse on key match; exact-candidate verdicts stay commit-keyed WO-02, WO-03 and WO-05 record the files and digests each finding depends on, so a candidate change re-runs only what moved
R7 Integer allocation quotas Largest-remainder apportionment over the rolling window Routing uses integer quotas and integer band bounds; admission and concurrency rules unchanged

The two-cost distinction that drives R1. The cost of determining whether a gate is satisfied is usually far below the cost of satisfying it. Gate 8 is the clearest case: the Long Table canary bundle either exists or it does not, and looking takes minutes, while producing it takes days. Determining it on Wednesday converts the largest known risk from a Saturday-noon surprise with eighteen hours left into a Wednesday decision with ninety.

What these rules do not do. They do not reduce the thirty evidence rows, the nine gates, or the attended minutes those require. They change the order in which unknowns are resolved, the amount of work repeated unnecessarily, and whether an unknown can be mistaken for a pass. No percentage reduction is claimed.

8. Stop rules

  • R2. A gate that is U is not a pass. Completion is computed from the ledger and never asserted; a triggered no-go is never overridden by a satisfied conjunction.
  • R3. Before any optional investigation, status check, review pass or provider call, name the decision it could flip. If no outcome changes any action, do not take it. Required security, CI, approval and exact-candidate checks are mandatory and outside this rule.
  • Deployment, publishing, merge, deletion, purchases, credential or access changes stop in OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED.
  • No lane accepts or merges its own material work; the Director does not accept code it authored.
  • Every spend is reserved against the USD 10 campaign, USD 50 daily and USD 14 task ceilings before it happens. Missing cost telemetry is unknown, never zero.
  • Ambiguous provider or effect outcomes go to reconciliation, never a blind retry.
  • No plaintext environment-file copies; protected-payload backups only.
  • Snapshot cleanliness is checked inside the container.
  • LIVE_OK older than twenty-four hours is stale: redo the chain.
  • Rollback is not a safety net. It refuses on ambiguous effects or non-quiescence, it never undoes an external effect, and the deployed script does not yet carry PR #115's correction.
  • One intake surface at a time: never two deployments on the same Slack channel and n8n instance.
