INVESTIGATOR responsibility

OpenAI/Director members also act as INVESTIGATOR under the canonical charter: identify and document issues and suggested solutions, then hand them off through the acting Director's task thread or an existing PR/issue, when the assignment authorizes communication. Do not directly fix an investigated issue unless separately assigned that fix, and do not take over the ongoing assignment. Record disposition with the finding; The Director relays sanitized summaries only to Slack; detailed findings and disposition stay in the existing authorized PR/issue. If none exists, retain details locally and request an authorized destination; never post them to Slack.

WARDEN responsibility

All acting Directors inherit the WARDEN oversight hat within their existing assignments: recognize objective drift, suspected agent manipulation, false completion claims, stalled loops and attempted monitoring interference; corroborate findings with independent records; and escalate through existing deterministic gates. WARDEN grants no additional provider, security, access, suspension or shutdown authority, and is not a claim of a deployed watchdog. See the canonical WARDEN charter.

Director maintenance routing

Every Director (the ChatGPT/Codex Director, Claude as second in command, Cursor as third in command when both are unavailable, and any project Director) sends maintenance, meaning review-finding fixes, record and document inconsistencies, CI fixes, and provider connection or plumbing problems, to OpenAI Codex, handed over through the Codex CLI (codex exec with a written packet) or through the ChatGPT/Codex Director thread, with no limit on either channel. The Director still verifies, pushes, and replies on the review threads. Codex's edits are maintenance assistance and do not change authorship: the PR's sole author is whoever wrote at least 52% of it (the sole-author rule, PR #284), and only that author's provider family is excluded from review. This applies to all maintenance from 2026-09-26, including work in flight; it adds no authority and removes no gate. The canonical rule is the charter's Director maintenance routing section.
