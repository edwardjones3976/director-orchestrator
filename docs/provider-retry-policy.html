Provider messages and jobs: four retries

Owner amendment, 24 October 2026. Director-Orchestrator only.

One initial attempt plus at most four retries means five total attempts. This is a ceiling. A successful operation stops immediately. A retry still needs an admissible failure, remaining resources and the original authority.

Where the budget applies
SurfaceRule
New provider messages and jobsFreeze five total attempts in the assignment and pre-stage packet before approval or dispatch. Record each actual attempt under the same message or assignment identity.
Existing signed grantsRetain their stricter limits and original recovery conditions. Legacy three-attempt packets and recorded digests are not rewritten. Missing historical authority does not become five attempts.
Fallback and changed transportUse the same consumed counters, absolute deadline and remaining resources. A new session or route alias does not renew an exhausted assignment.
Native clients and SDKsKeep nested retries disabled when the Director owns the outer attempt budget. Independent retry layers must not multiply five into twenty-five. A transport's stricter no-repost or reconciliation gate remains binding.
Repairs and revisionsTheir separate limits remain unchanged. Four provider retries do not authorize four source edits, CI reruns, reviews, merges or deployments.

Before each retry

  1. Read the persisted attempt and effect history. Stop on success. An active or ambiguous paid request requires authenticated reconciliation before any replay.
  2. Classify the failure. A definite transient refusal can retry within the bound. A nontransient later attempt requires new evidence or a materially corrected approach; unchanged permission and authentication refusals are not temporary failures.
  3. Recheck cancellation, deadline, token and cost bounds, provider eligibility and required approval. Unknown cost or missing accounting is not zero.
  4. Reserve the next ordinal before I/O. Never reset history, change a packet digest to obtain a fresh allowance, or hide fallback calls outside the count.
  5. Read back and retain the result. On exhaustion, mark unavailable for that assignment and follow the authorized fallback or explicit blocker path.

Implementation and installation boundaries

The shared policy constants are MAX_PROVIDER_RETRIES = 4 and MAX_PROVIDER_ATTEMPTS = 5. Prospective planners explicitly freeze the new value. Historical model defaults retain three, preventing an old omitted field from silently widening authority. The primary dispatcher and Postgres admission use the assignment's stored limit, including after restart. Qualification and scorecard readers carry that same stored three-or-five limit through their typed readback and retry validation. Missing or coerced limits fail closed; reacquire an older readback rather than inventing authority.

SQLite schema widening copies existing attempt and outcome rows in one transaction and restores append-only guards. Postgres setup widens only the numeric constraints; it does not change stored grant values or reset ordinals. These code paths are exercised with disposable test databases. Production migration or deployment still requires its existing release authority.

Local provider wrappers that submit one bounded native call keep their hidden client retries at zero. The Foreman tracks additional authorized messages through the existing receipts and work order. A connection test, source merge or policy document alone does not prove that a long-running client has reloaded settings.
