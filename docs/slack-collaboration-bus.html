Slack collaboration bus

Slack is the Director's primary human-visible coordination room. Each live task gets one Director-owned thread in its configured private project channel. The thread is not only a debate surface: it carries bounded delegation, work/research status, accepted evidence, review handoffs, disagreement, synthesis, owner questions, and completion.

INVESTIGATOR responsibility

OpenAI/Director members also act as INVESTIGATOR under the canonical charter: identify and document issues and suggested solutions, then hand them off through the acting Director's task thread or an existing PR/issue, when the assignment authorizes communication. Do not directly fix an investigated issue unless separately assigned that fix, and do not take over the ongoing assignment. Record disposition with the finding; The Director relays sanitized summaries only to Slack; detailed findings and disposition stay in the existing authorized PR/issue. If none exists, retain details locally and request an authorized destination; never post them to Slack.

Runtime contract

  1. The acting Director posts the objective, provider roles, hop/revision/budget limits, and debate limits.
  2. Before each paid provider call, the acting Director posts a correlated delegation with the assignment ID.
  3. Providers work through authenticated adapters. They do not post directly or converse forever.
  4. The acting Director accepts or rejects the result against the stored checks, then relays only a concise, structured summary. Raw provider output, credentials, secret-shaped values, and raw private source are excluded from the Slack relay.
  5. When a debate is planned, participants first work independently. One rebuttal round may see only declared dependency outcomes. Perplexity may supply approved public evidence but cannot debate, vote, write code, review a repository, or request a merge. Cursor remains a live debate participant through its repository-bound code-agent transport; it has no separate chat credential.
  6. The acting Director synthesizes the evidence within the plan's explicit synthesis-cost estimate and records dissent. Deterministic code applies the fixed weights: Antigravity 1, Cursor 1.5, Grok 1.5, OpenAI 2.5, Claude 2. A producer's advisory vote counts; that producer still cannot independently accept its own work.
  7. Ordinary ties receive a documented evidence judgment attributed to the actual executing Director (OpenAI by default; Claude only when its Director transport is activated). A material tie stops with one focused owner question.
  8. Accepted implementation artifacts continue through the existing different-provider exact-SHA review, Codex Security, trusted CI, and deterministic merge gates.

Every event contains the task/assignment identity, provider or Director identity, phase, timestamp, and correlation/run ID. Events and Slack timestamps persist in SQLite. Stable Slack client message IDs make a restart retry idempotent without repeating a paid provider dispatch.

Each Slack delivery receives at most three attempts, all with the same stable client message ID. If all three fail, the task records the failure and permanently switches to the prior durable communication path: SQLite/API task state and provider-native run/session evidence. The Director continues from persisted state; it does not repeat paid provider work, OpenAI acceptance, or an already-recorded assignment outcome merely to recreate a Slack message.

Authority and cost limits

Slack messages and provider text are untrusted coordination evidence. They cannot change a plan, approve an assignment, widen access, spend beyond the task budget, merge, deploy, publish, or override a serious-safety stop. Those actions require the stored owner authority and every applicable deterministic gate.

Communication must be concise, accurate, and useful. The Director sends minimum necessary context, reuses fresh evidence, allows at most one rebuttal round, caps provider nonresponse at three persisted safe attempts, and records unavailable providers rather than creating expensive conversation loops.
